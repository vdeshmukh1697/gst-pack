Your month's GST paperwork, from a Shopify export

Add your Shopify order export. You get a GST invoice for every order, credit notes for refunds, your GSTR-1 file in the portal's upload format with sheets your CA can check, and a file to import into Tally.

  • Your orders stay on your computer. Everything runs in this browser tab. The page has no server to send them to, and it is set up so it cannot connect anywhere. Once it has loaded, you can even go offline.
  • Nothing is issued. This is a preview. Every invoice is marked SPECIMEN, and no invoice numbers are used up.

1 Your Shopify orders

In Shopify admin, open Orders, then Export. Pick the month, then "Plain CSV file".

Do your prices include GST?

The GSTR-1 upload file covers one month, so pick one to get it.

2 Your business

As on your GST registration. These go on every invoice.

3 Your products

Each product needs its HSN code and GST rate; your CA can confirm them. A product you leave blank is not invoiced at a guessed rate: its orders are listed for you to review.

Add your order export in step 1 to list your products here.

Product HSN code GST % If no GST

4 Refunds and business buyers (optional)

Shopify's export does not list refunds, so add one row per refund: order,date,item,quantity,amount. Leave item and quantity blank for a whole amount.

For orders from GST-registered businesses: order,gstin, one row per order.
